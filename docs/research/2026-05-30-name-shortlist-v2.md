## Three leads (all pass the generic-collision test)

### 1. Calluna  *(metaphor — heather)*
Botanical: heather, the hardy moorland plant that regenerates on bare ground → **resilience / agency**, soft and gentle. **Cleanest combination** of a clear wellness trademark lane + on-brand meaning + no same-class product collision.
- **Shadow:** the well-known *Calluna* typeface (exljbris/Adobe), an indie Steam game, a hackathon project — none are wellness apps or SaaS brands in our class (typefaces ≠ app trademark scope).
- **Domain:** bare `calluna.com` may be parked/held → lockup `calluna.app` (primary) or `usecalluna.com`/`callunaapp.com`.
- **Watch-out:** confirm the typeface owner holds no class 9/42 software mark.
- **Taglines:** "Map your trauma. Grow your way through it." · "A private, local-first space to map trauma and find steadier ground."

### 2. Sennen  *(metaphor — a calm Cornish cove)*
Reads as a real, human, calming place + a soft given name → "safe harbor / calm coast" without using ruled-out Harbor/Anchor words. **Most emotionally on-brand.**
- **Shadow:** Sennen Tech (renewable-energy SaaS) and a Sennen property-management app — unrelated classes; wellness lane clear.
- **Domain:** bare `sennen.com` likely held (place name) → `sennen.app` / `getsennen.com` / `sennen.health`.
- **Watch-out:** confirm no overlap of Sennen Tech into health class 9/42 (likely fine; get counsel's read).
- **Taglines:** "Find calm ground. Map trauma at your own pace — privately." · "Your safe harbor for mapping trauma, fully local and encrypted."

### 3. Velora  *(coined — hedge)*
Invented, soft, easy to say/spell; coined words clear trademarks and get `.com`/`.app` far more easily (the Calm/Daylio/Finch pattern). **Easiest to own end-to-end.**
- **Domain:** test bare `velora.com`; fallback `velora.app` / `veloraapp.com` / `getvelora.com`.
- **Watch-out:** "Vel-" appears in beauty/cosmetics — confirm no live class 44/3 conflict.
- **Taglines:** "Gently map your trauma — a private, local-first map of you." · "See the connections. Reclaim the story. Trauma mapping that stays yours."
