# Calluna — Deep Clearance Verification

**Date:** 2026-05-30 · Deep verification of the shortlist lead "Calluna". Preliminary sanity check, **not legal advice**. Web-checked May 2026; flagged items need official-source confirmation.

## Verdict: **CAUTION**

The *software* trademark lane is plausibly open and the app stores are empty — so it's usable with effort and qualifiers. But "Calluna" is a **contested, recycled name**: dominated in search by a famous typeface, crowded with wellness/holistic businesses, shadowed by a funded health-sector pharma, with the **`.com` and every prime social handle already taken**. Not a clean, distinctive, ownable name for a sensitive mental-health product.

## What's good
- **No live US trademark for bare "CALLUNA" in software (class 9/42).** The one on-point mark (Reg 4219990, incl. "design and development of computer software") is **DEAD — cancelled 2019**.
- **App stores clean** — no "Calluna" app on Apple or Google Play (only fuzzy near-spellings).
- **Modern domain lockups open** — `calluna.app`, `.io`, `.health`, `usecalluna.com`, `getcalluna.com` all show NXDOMAIN (likely registrable; verify at registrar).
- Tonally lovely: soft, calm, gentle — a good *feel* for the product.

## What's not (the skeptic's list)
1. **The typeface owns search.** Calluna is a well-known Adobe Fonts/exljbris serif; "Calluna" results are saturated with font sites. Perpetual SEO drag — you'll always need a qualifier ("Calluna app"). *This is the most certain practical harm.* (TM blocking risk from the font is LOW — it's ™ not ®, and a different goods lane.)
2. **Calluna Pharma** — funded Norwegian biotech (~€75–82M, Phase 2), health-adjacent. Biggest sector collision + a likely future trademark watcher.
3. **Live "CALLUNA SPA" trademark** (class 44, wellness) — a citable near-neighbor for a wellness app (weakened by the disclaimed "SPA" + goods difference, but real).
4. **Crowded wellness field already** — Calluna Labs (CBD), Calluna Wellness (meditation/Reiki), Calluna Holistics, Calluna Natural Health. You wouldn't be "the" Calluna in wellness.
5. **Prime social handles taken** — `@calluna` is taken on Instagram, X, and **GitHub** (matters for the repo/org rename). Forced into lockups (`@getcalluna`, `@callunaapp`) everywhere → consistency/discovery cost.
6. **`calluna.com` is held** (placeholder/holding page) — start on a compromise TLD or negotiate a purchase.
7. **Spelling-on-hearing shaky** — "Caluna" (one L) is already a wellness competitor (typo-collision).
8. **The resilience story is obscure** — etymology is Greek *kallýnō* = "to beautify/sweep" (heather → brooms), **not** resilience. The "hardy plant → resilience" angle must be taught; it doesn't tell itself.
9. **Name still being actively claimed** — a brand-new CALLUNA filing (class 41) on 2026-05-20.

## The pattern (important)
Two deep verifications, two CAUTIONs:
- **Mendmap** → fatal "mindmap" generic collision.
- **Calluna** → recycled/crowded, typeface owns SEO, .com + handles gone.

Evocative dictionary/botanical names keep coming back **crowded or SEO-dominated**. The reliably *ownable* path for a brand that needs the `.com` + matching handles is a **coined/invented word** (the Calm/Daylio/Finch pattern) — which is exactly the shortlist's coined hedge, **Velora**.

## Recommendation
Don't commit Calluna. Pivot to the **coined** route — verify **Velora** next (or run a fresh coined-only round pre-screened for domain + handle availability), since coined words avoid the existing-company / typeface / SEO problems that sank the dictionary picks. If you love Calluna's feel, it's usable only with a standardized qualifier everywhere (e.g., `getcalluna` / `calluna.app`) and a trademark-counsel pass on the live CALLUNA SPA mark.
