Context: rebranding away from "TrauMapp'd" (collides with existing TraumApp/Traumapp; apostrophe-d unusable in URLs; "trauma" in-name is stigmatizing). Target: a map/connection metaphor with a safe/calm tone; "trauma" goes in the **tagline** for SEO.

## Ranked recommendation

1. **Mendmap** — *mend + map*. **Best clearance + best domain outlook of everything tested.** No exact "Mendmap" user found in software, wellness, or app stores. Spells-on-hearing, self-explanatory, non-clinical. Caveat: the "Mend" family is crowded (Mend breakup-recovery app `letsmend.com`; Mend.io app-security; Mend.com telemedicine) — but the *fused compound* is far more defensible than bare "Mend." Minor brand knock: "mend" implies something was broken (deficit-framed) vs. growth/resilience. **Lead candidate.**
2. **Plotline** — *plot points on a map + the plotline of your story*. Cleanest *fresh* concept, warmer/growth-oriented tone. No wellness or mind-map app found under it. Caveat: an existing **martech/product-analytics "Plotline"** (different class) needs a proper search; exact `.com`/`.app` may need a lockup. **Strong #2 — arguably #1 on tone if domains clear.**
3. **Constellate** — keep only if you love the constellation metaphor *and* legal clears around **Constella** (a same-category "Digital Brain" PKM app at `constella.app`) and **Constellate Health** (an App Store health app). Higher risk than the top two.

## Why the strong-concept names were down-ranked

- **Cairn** (fit 5/5) — most crowded name tested: software/SaaS (Cairn Applications, Cairn Software, Cairnstack) **and** wellness (**Cairn Companion** — "your private mental wellness companion," near-identical positioning — plus Cairn Mental Health/Psychology/Counseling/Recovery). `cairn.com` unobtainable; spelling-on-hearing fails (Karen/Cairne). Gorgeous but a clearance + SEO minefield.
- **Constellate/Constella** — `constella.app` is a live **same-category** PKM app (the most dangerous kind of collision); **Constellate Health** is a same-class App Store app; "Constellation Software/Energy" broaden crowding.
- **Rejected directions:** weaving (Weft/Loomly/LoomLine), inner-atlas (Inner Atlas, Innermap — both occupied in-space), path/navigation (Wayfinder, Trailhead, ThroughLine — all saturated in wellness; ThroughLine is the largest crisis-helpline network), light (Lantern was a defunct CBT app; Lumen jammed), anchor (Tether, Solace — saturated).

## Verify before relying (do before committing)

1. **USPTO** (tmsearch.uspto.gov) — Mendmap, Plotline, Constellate/Constella, Cairn + phonetic equivalents, in **Class 9** (apps), **42** (SaaS), **44** (health/wellness), **41** (self-help content). Note the live **Constellate** mark + **Constellate Health** app.
2. **EUIPO** (euipo.europa.eu) — same names if marketing in the EU; check **Constella Intelligence** (EU-active).
3. **Common-law/web marks** (not in registries): Cairn Companion, Mend (letsmend.com), constella.app, Innermap, Thrum haptic — have counsel weigh confusion risk.
4. **Domains at a registrar** — authoritative WHOIS for `.com`/`.app`/`.io` of the finalist + fallback lockups (`get-`, `-app`, `try-`).
5. **App stores** — search Apple App Store + Google Play for the exact finalist *today* (Health/Fitness, Lifestyle).
6. **Social handles** — X/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/YouTube/Reddit for the exact name + lockup, in one sitting.
7. **Trademark attorney** — formal clearance/availability opinion before filing/launch, especially given the crowding around Cairn/Constella(te)/Mend.

**Bottom line:** lead with **Mendmap** (clearance + domains) or **Plotline** (tone + dual metaphor, pending the adjacent-martech check); treat Cairn/Constellate/Constella as high-risk despite strong concepts; keep "trauma" in the tagline.
