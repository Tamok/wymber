# Mendmap — Deep Clearance Verification

**Date:** 2026-05-30 · Follow-up to the name-clearance shortlist (which picked Mendmap as lead). Triggered by "research the chosen name before moving too fast."

> Preliminary skeptical sanity check, **not legal advice**. Jan 2026 cutoff + May 2026 web checks. Several authoritative sources (USPTO TSDR, EUIPO, Justia, registrar WHOIS, X) blocked automated access — those are flagged "verify at official source."

## Recommendation: **CAUTION** (not GO)

The exact term "Mendmap" appears genuinely unclaimed as a trademark, app, or product, and it's pronounceable and on-brand — real upside. But **two material risks keep it out of GO**, and one is serious enough to reconsider the name entirely.

### 🔴 Risk 1 — "Mendmap" ≈ "Mindmap" (the generic category term)
This is the standout finding. The name is **one phoneme/one letter from "mindmap"** — the generic word for the exact kind of product we're building (a mind-map-style tool). Consequences:
- Chronic "is it Mind or Mend?" confusion; mis-typed URLs; voice assistants/autocorrect "fixing" it to "mind map."
- **SEO drag:** "mendmap" has near-zero competition (easy to rank) but sits adjacent to the ultra-competitive "mindmap" keyword — organic discovery bleeds into generic mind-mapping results, and we lose typo traffic.
- Ironic for a *mapping* app specifically. **Recommend a real "say it to 10 people, count how many write 'Mindmap'" test before committing.**

### 🔴 Risk 2 — crowded "Mend" mental-health field
The "mend = emotional healing" metaphor is already a wellness naming convention, and two strong occupants sit in our adjacent niche:
- **letsmend.com (Mend)** — breakup/heartbreak-recovery wellness app + courses. Closest conceptual neighbor.
- **mend.com** — HIPAA behavioral/mental-health telemedicine, ~400k visits/mo, well-funded.
- **mendapp.io** — an existing mental-health app on the "Mend" stem.
- Mend.io (AppSec) even ships an "AppSec **Map**" and its scanners emit `mendmap*` strings.
- Live **MEND** trademark registrations exist in classes 41/44 (our wellness/self-help classes) — social-services, conceptually distant, but they narrow a clean class-44 runway.

→ A fused "Mendmap" in a different sub-niche is *more* distinctive than "Mend [Word]," so coexistence is plausible — but likelihood-of-confusion / opposition exposure is **non-trivial**, especially vs. letsmend and mend.com.

### 🟠 Risk 3 — `mendmap.com` is already taken
Resolves to a live "Welcome to Reclaim Hosting" default page → **owned, undeveloped** (not obviously for sale). The premium `.com` isn't free. `mendmap.app` / `.io` returned connection-refused (no live site — *not* a confirmation they're registrable). Verify at a registrar.

### Other
- App stores: **no exact "Mendmap"** on Apple/Google — but a sea of "Mend" apps to be confused with.
- Social: no `@mendmap` surfaced (weak signal; verify + grab if open).
- General web: no real company named Mendmap (only the owned domain + Mend.io scanner test artifacts + a trivial stock illustration).
- Linguistics otherwise good; no negative meanings found.

## Verify-at-official-source checklist (before buying/renaming/filing)
1. USPTO full-text + phonetic search: MENDMAP, MEND MAP, and MEND in classes 9/41/42/44 (+ TSDR status).
2. EUIPO same search for priority markets.
3. Trademark counsel opinion specifically vs. **letsmend (Mend)** and **mend.com**.
4. State business-registry + exact-match web check for unregistered "Mendmap" use.
5. Registrar WHOIS/price for mendmap.com (owner / for-sale?), .app, .io, .co, getmendmap.com, mendmapapp.com.
6. Live Apple App Store + Google Play search right before launch.
7. Manually confirm + reserve @mendmap on X/IG/TikTok/LinkedIn/YouTube/Reddit if open.
8. **User confusion test:** say the name to 10+ people; count how many write "Mindmap." A high fraction = strong NO-GO.

## Bottom line
Don't lock Mendmap yet. The "mindmap" collision is a genuine, structural liability for a mapping product, and the "Mend" mental-health field is crowded. Either (a) pivot to a name without a generic-term collision (e.g., **Plotline**, the clearance runner-up), or (b) proceed with Mendmap only after the checklist clears AND the mind/mend user test comes back clean.
