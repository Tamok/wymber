# Velora — Deep Clearance Verification

**Date:** 2026-05-31 · Deep verification of the coined lead "Velora". Preliminary sanity check, **not legal advice**. Web-checked 2026-05-31; some registries (USPTO TSDR, Trademarkia) were access-blocked and are flagged "verify."

## Verdict: **NO-GO**

The disqualifier: **"Velora by ieso" is a live, VC-backed AI *mental-health* app** (anxiety/stress/burnout), on **both the Apple App Store and Google Play**, by Ieso Digital Health (Cambridge UK). That's a direct, same-vertical, same-name competitor — a structural likelihood-of-confusion problem, not a fixable one. Everything else independently reinforces NO-GO.

## Why (the cluster)
- **ieso "Velora™"** — AI mental-wellness app, funded, clinical data, app-store presence, privacy-claiming. *Fatal-grade collision.*
- **Velora Studios, LLC** — owns **velora.com** and a **registered USPTO mark** (Reg. 3947378, digital design/hosting). The flagship domain is gone and held by a mark holder.
- **More health apps named Velora:** Velora Health Companion (iOS fitness, on-device/privacy-first), Velora Care (clinic aftercare), Velora (PL psychophysiology). App stores are saturated in our exact categories; near-spelling **Valera Health** (a behavioral-health app) compounds confusion.
- **Trademark crowding** across classes 9/42/44 (software/health), 42 (design, registered), 21 (housewares, filed 2024), and a dense beauty/cosmetics swarm (class 3).
- **Domains:** velora.com/.ai/.xyz/.care all taken; only awkward lockups remain.
- **Handles/GitHub:** base @velora taken on IG/X/TikTok; **github.com/Velora is taken** (Velora Studios) — a clean repo/org rename is unavailable.
- **Connotation:** dominant association is **feminine beauty/skincare**, which undercuts gravitas for a trauma product.

## The 3-strike pattern (important)
| Name | Type | Verdict | Killer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mendmap | coined-ish | CAUTION | homophone of "mindmap" |
| Calluna | botanical | CAUTION | typeface owns SEO; pharma + spa marks; .com/handles taken |
| **Velora** | coined | **NO-GO** | **live same-vertical mental-health app (ieso)** |

**Lesson:** generating a creative name and *then* checking availability keeps failing — attractive tokens (especially in mental health) are already taken. The process has to **invert to availability-first**: pre-screen each candidate against (1) app-store collision in Health/Wellness, (2) `.com` obtainability, (3) GitHub org, (4) same-class trademark — and only surface names that already pass. Note that *surviving* MH app names tend to be **deliberately distinctive/quirky** (Daylio, Bearable, Finch, Sanvello, Wysa, Woebot), not elegant Latinate words.

## Recommendation
Drop Velora. Either (a) run **one availability-first round** that pre-verifies app-store + `.com` + GitHub + trademark for each distinctive/quirky coinage *before* presenting, so the shortlist is already vetted; or (b) **park naming** and keep building the MVP under the working name, revisiting later (or with a professional naming/clearance service). The name does not block development.
