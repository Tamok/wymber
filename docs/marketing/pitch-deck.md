### 1 — Title
**TrauMapp'd**
*See your healing take shape.*
> Soft hero visual (the map screenshot, dimmed). Tagline only. Calm.

### 2 — The moment
> "Connect the dots between your triggers, your coping, your people." Survivors hear this constantly — with no safe, private place to actually do it.
**Speaker note:** open with the lived gap, not the product.

### 3 — The problem
- Trauma is **nonlinear & relational** → journals (linear) don't fit.
- The data is **the most sensitive there is** → cloud-by-default apps are a poor fit.
- People in distress need **calm, predictable, accessible** tools → most aren't.

### 4 — The solution
**A trauma-informed map of your experience.** Events, emotions, people, places, triggers, coping, insight, growth — as nodes you arrange and connect.
> Show the map. One sentence: "Your experience, made visible — and entirely yours."

### 5 — Why it's different
1. **Mapping, not journaling.**
2. **Genuinely private** (local-first + zero-knowledge sync).
3. **Trauma-informed by design.**
4. **You own it** (export, no lock-in).

### 6 — Privacy is the architecture (not a promise)
- Data lives **on your device**, encrypted with a key only you hold.
- Optional sync is **end-to-end encrypted** — the server stores ciphertext it *cannot* read.
- Optional AI runs **on-device** — your story never leaves.
**Speaker note:** the privacy claim is literally the system design. That's the moat.

### 7 — Trauma-informed & accessible
Gentle language · soft palette · predictable UI · no jarring motion · crisis resources always reachable · **accessibility built into the architecture** (keyboard-first, screen-reader-aware, WCAG AA).

### 8 — Product demo
> Live or GIF: add a node → connect two nodes → reload (it persists) → "Analyze" surfaces gentle patterns → export. 
*(MVP milestone M1 hardens persistence, canvas theming, and onboarding.)*

### 9 — The optional AI companion (later)
A **private, on-device** "Explore" mode that gently helps you notice patterns and *suggests* possible connections — never directive, always opt-in, crisis-aware, zero data egress. A flagship differentiator at near-zero marginal cost.

### 10 — Market & audience
- Primary: adults in self-directed healing (often alongside therapy).
- Secondary: therapists/coaches who recommend a tool clients fully control.
- Tailwinds: mental-health self-tools ↑, data-harvesting distrust ↑, on-device AI now viable.
> Detailed competitive landscape: see research report (in progress).

### 11 — Business model (low overhead)
- **Free, local-first** core (desktop + web; mobile later).
- **Paid:** encrypted cross-device **sync**. Recurring, low-cost; zero-knowledge server = low cost & liability.
- Optional later: desktop license / supporter tier / private-AI pack.

### 12 — Roadmap
- **M0** Foundations & hardening · **M1** MVP (persistent map, safe accounts, trauma-informed UX, desktop+PWA) · **M2** encrypted sync (paid) · **M3** Android→iOS · **Backlog** private-AI Explore/Connect, DBT/mindfulness modules.

### 13 — Ask / Close
**The tool we wished existed: private, gentle, and yours.**
> CTA depends on context (early users, collaborators, or funding). Keep it warm, not salesy.

## Design direction for the rendered deck
- Palette: the app's soft pastels (sage, soft blue, lavender). Avoid red/alarm colors.
- Type: humanist sans, large, airy. High contrast (AA) but not stark.
- Imagery: abstract/soft; **no** distressing trauma imagery. Real product screenshots over stock.
- Motion (if any): slow, gentle fades only — mirror the product's `prefers-reduced-motion` ethic.
