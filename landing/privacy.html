Privacy policy

Most privacy policies explain what a company does with your data. Wymber is built so there is no data to do anything with. This page says that precisely, in plain language.

What we collect: nothing

  • There are no accounts. You never give us a name, an email, or any sign-up detail.
  • There is no analytics or telemetry in the app. We don't track what you do, and we set no cookies.
  • There is no server-side storage of user data. Nothing you write is ever transmitted to us.

Where your map lives

Your map is stored in a single encrypted vault on your own device, in your browser's private storage. It is sealed with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password (or your recovery code). Only ciphertext is ever at rest. We cannot read your map, reset your password, or recover your data, because we never have any of it. That is a design choice, not a promise: the code is open source, so anyone can verify it.

What this means in practice

  • If you clear your browser's site data, your vault is deleted with it. Your recovery sheet protects you from a forgotten password, not from deleted storage, so consider keeping an export if your map matters to you.
  • Your map never syncs anywhere today. If optional encrypted sync arrives later, it will be opt-in and zero-knowledge: only the sealed file would travel, and we would still never hold your key. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.
  • Crisis links (call or text 988, 911) use your phone's dialer or messaging app directly. Nothing about using them touches us.

This website

wymber.app is a set of static pages served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes requests (such as IP addresses) transiently to deliver the site and protect it from abuse; see the Cloudflare privacy policy. We run no trackers and no advertising, here or in the app.

AI and search engines

Your map can never be read by anyone, including AI systems: it is encrypted on your device. The public pages of this website may be indexed and cited by search engines and AI assistants, and we ask AI companies not to use them for model training (declared machine-readably in our robots.txt Content Signals).

If you email us

If you write to jonathan@wymber.app, we see your email address and what you send, and we use it only to reply. Please don't include anything from your map that you'd rather keep private.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be visible in the changelog and in the site's public history. The policy can only get more specific about the same promise: your map stays yours.
