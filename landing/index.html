Privacy, for real

Most apps promise privacy. Wymber is built so that we can't see your map, even if we wanted to.

It never leaves your device

There's no account and no server to sign in to. Wymber runs in your browser and keeps your map locally.

Encrypted with a key only you hold

Your words are sealed with a key derived from your password. There's nothing on our end to read, because there's no "our end."

A recovery sheet, not a dead end

Forgetting a password shouldn't cost you your story. Keep a printable recovery code so your map is always yours to reopen.

Open-source, so it outlives us

Wymber's code is public. Even if this site disappeared, you could still open your .wymber file with the open code. Your data is never locked to us.

Sync, when you want it soon

Optional encrypted sync is on the way. Even then, only the sealed file travels, and we never hold your key. Zero-knowledge by design.

Designed to stay out of your way

Predictable, reversible, and yours to control. In practice:

  • Soft colours and unhurried transitions. Nothing jarring.
  • Esc always closes what's open. You're never trapped in a screen.
  • Three themes: light, dark, and a low-contrast soft mode.
  • Built to WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard-navigable, screen-reader friendly, and it honours reduced-motion.
