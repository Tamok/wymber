About Wymber

Wymber exists because laying things out, what happened, how it connects, how you're growing, can make a tangled story easier to hold. And because that kind of map is exactly the data that should never sit on someone else's server.

What it is

Wymber is a private mapping tool in the lineage of paper exercises like the trauma egg and the trauma timeline: you put down dots for the events, feelings, people, triggers, coping, and growth that matter, and connect them at your own pace. It is a self-help tool for personal reflection, not therapy, diagnosis, or medical care, and the design choices behind it are documented openly in our sources.

The principles

  • Local-first, privacy-first. Your map is encrypted on your device. No accounts, no telemetry, nothing transmitted. See the privacy policy, which is short because there's nothing to disclose.
  • Trauma-informed. Gentle language, soft colours, predictable UI, nothing urgent, everything reversible. Built to WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard-first.
  • Open-source. The code is public (AGPL-3.0), so the privacy claims are auditable rather than asking for trust. Even if this site disappeared, your map would still open with the open code.

Who makes it

Wymber is built by Zoignon Studio, a small independent software studio, and developed in the open on GitHub. It started as a personal project to map a difficult story, and grew into a tool worth sharing. It is currently in alpha: early, changing often, and shaped by the trauma-informed research it's grounded in.
