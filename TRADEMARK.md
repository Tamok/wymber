# Wymber Brand, Trademark & Content Policy

Wymber's **code** is free and open source under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). This file
covers the things the code license does **not** grant: the Wymber **name**, its **logo and
visual identity**, and the **written content** (the copy and prose on the landing site and in
the docs). Those belong to **Zoignon Studio LLC** and are **not** licensed under AGPL-3.0.

This is the ordinary open-source split. You are free to use, modify, and redistribute the
*code*; the *brand* stays ours so that "Wymber" always means the real thing. Open source does
not make a name or logo free to use (see the OSI's guidance on trademarks in open source).

Per AGPL-3.0 section 7(e), we decline to grant any rights under trademark law for use of our
trademarks.

## What this covers

- **The name** "Wymber" (and "wymber.app") as a word mark.
- **The logo, wordmark, favicon, and Open Graph / share cards:** `landing/logo.png`,
  `landing/og.png`, `landing/favicon.svg`, `frontend/favicon.svg`, `frontend/og-image.png`,
  and the screenshots in `landing/screenshots/`.
- **The visual identity:** the specific palette, layout, and overall look of wymber.app as a
  coordinated brand.
- **The written content:** the prose and copy on the landing site (`landing/`) and in the
  project docs, as a creative work. (The code those pages are built from stays AGPL-3.0.)

## What you can do without asking (nominative / fair use)

- Refer to Wymber by name, truthfully: "based on Wymber", "a fork of Wymber", "reads Wymber
  export files".
- Redistribute the **official, unmodified** app with its branding intact.
- Quote the content, and use screenshots and the logo, to **write about, review, teach, or
  report on** Wymber.
- Link to [wymber.app](https://wymber.app).

## What needs our permission

- Using the **Wymber name or logo for a modified version, fork, or a competing or derived
  product or service.** If you fork the code (which AGPL lets you do), give your version its
  own name and logo.
- Using the name, logo, or content in a way that **implies endorsement or affiliation, or that
  your version is the official Wymber.**
- Selling merchandise or services under the Wymber name or logo.
- Reusing the **written content** outside the fair-use cases above (for example, lifting the
  landing copy for another product).

## Forking, the friendly version

We *want* the code to be forkable; that is the point of AGPL. If you fork: keep the code open
(AGPL requires it) and **rebrand.** Pick your own name, swap the logo and the share cards, and
adjust the copy. A credit like "based on Wymber (wymber.app)" is welcome; that is nominative
use. The one thing we ask is that nobody can mistake your version for the official one, because
people trust Wymber with sensitive data and that trust rests on knowing what they are running.
