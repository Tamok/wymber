# What's changed in Wymber

A short, human list of what's new, so you don't have to go looking. For the full technical
history, see the [commits](https://github.com/Tamok/wymber/commits).

## 2026-06-06
- Two nodes connect at most once now, no more accidental duplicate links.
- Unlink connections: open a node's Connections to remove a link, or tap the line between two.
- Crisis support numbers are tap-to-call and tap-to-text.
- A short, skippable "How it works" walkthrough you can reopen anytime.
- This changelog, so changes aren't hidden away.

## 2026-06-04
- The app is now its own space at web.wymber.app; the landing stays at wymber.app.
- A show/hide toggle on password fields.
- Esc reliably closes whatever is open.

## Earlier
- Private, local-first trauma mapping: an encrypted vault that lives on your device, a graph you
  build at your own pace, grounding and crisis support always within reach, installable and fully
  offline.
