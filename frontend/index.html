You're not alone

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out — help is available right now.

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text, 24/7
  • Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line
  • 911 — Emergency services (US)
  • Find a helpline in your country — 175+ countries (needs internet)

Wymber can't contact anyone for you — these are external services.
