    You're not alone

    If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out. Help is available right now, and you deserve it.

    • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text, any time, day or night
    • Text HOME to 741741 Crisis Text Line
    • 911 Emergency services (US)
    • Find a helpline in your country 175+ countries (needs internet)

    Wymber can't contact anyone for you. These are external services you reach out to directly.
